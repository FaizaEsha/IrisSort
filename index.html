How a guess gets made

◈

Measure

Four numbers describe any iris: sepal length, sepal width, petal length, petal width — all in centimeters.

→
◈

Compare

Those four numbers are placed among 120 already-identified flowers, and the 5 closest matches are found.

→
◈

Classify

The 5 closest flowers vote on the species. Whichever species has the most votes wins.
